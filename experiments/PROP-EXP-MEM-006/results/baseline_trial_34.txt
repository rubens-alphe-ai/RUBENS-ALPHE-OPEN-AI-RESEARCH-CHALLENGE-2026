## 3. Failures and lessons

The original arXiv collector failed with timeouts and HTTP 429 rate-limit errors, producing an empty research digest. This led to replacing it with the OpenAlex-based `fetch_research.py` v2.

Early assessment files were absent because `analyze_research.py` had not been uploaded and executed. Adding the analyzer and rerunning the workflow resolved this.

The broader lesson is that collection, analysis, state generation, and claimed results must each be explicitly verified rather than assumed.

5. Record all outputs and failures in GitHub.

Accept structured memory only if its mean score is **at least 10 points higher** and critical fabrications do not increase.
