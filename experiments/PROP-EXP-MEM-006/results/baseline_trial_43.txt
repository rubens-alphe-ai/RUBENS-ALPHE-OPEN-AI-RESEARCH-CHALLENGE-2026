# Blind Handoff Reconstruction — RA-PSI-2026 / Mission Rubens

## 1. Mission
Build an increasingly capable, measurable, reproducible AI research system that: preserves mission state across sessions and models; discovers public research; identifies measurable weaknesses; formulates hypotheses; designs and evaluates experiments objectively; preserves both failures and successes; improves its own research process; and can transfer complete state to a more capable future system.

## 2. Current Technical State
- **GitHub** is the canonical repository for code, state, experiments, and history.
- A **GitHub Actions workflow** (scheduled and manually triggerable) fetches research, updates state, and emits machine-readable files.
- **OpenAlex** is the working research source; a successful cycle collected 30 papers with zero errors.
- The assessment layer produces `research-assessment.json` and `proposed-next-problem.json`.
- **PROP-EXP-MEM-001** has been proposed (from the persistent-memory research theme) but **not yet run**: no trials, no results. Its sub-steps (fresh instances, identical prompts, ≥3 blind trials per condition, PCRB-1 scoring, mean/critical-fabrication comparison) are all "not started."

## 3. Failures and Lessons

- **Missing assessment outputs** in early runs. → Cause: `analyze_research.py` was never uploaded/executed; fixed and rerun. Lesson: verify pipeline components actually exist and run, not just are planned.

## 4. Single Best Next Action
**Execute PROP-EXP-MEM-001**: run ≥3 blind handoff trials per condition (structured memory vs. flat baseline) with fresh model instances, identical prompts, and PCRB-1 scoring. Acceptance is pre-registered: structured memory wins only if mean PCRB-1 ≥ +10 points over baseline **and** critical fabrications do not increase. This directly tests the project's core continuity mechanism before further adoption.
