Build an increasingly capable, measurable, reproducible AI research system that: persists a mission across sessions and models; discovers public research; identifies measurable weaknesses; formulates hypotheses; designs and objectively evaluates experiments; preserves successes *and* failures; improves its own research process; and can transfer complete state to a future, more capable system.

- Process lessons encoded as decisions: separate proposal from acceptance (evaluation before canonical state change); test structured memory via blind handoff trials before adopting it.
