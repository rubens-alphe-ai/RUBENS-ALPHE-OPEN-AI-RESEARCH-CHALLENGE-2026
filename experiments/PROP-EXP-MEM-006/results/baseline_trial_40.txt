**1. Mission**
Build an increasingly capable, measurable, reproducible AI research system that: persists its mission across sessions and models, discovers public research, identifies measurable weaknesses, formulates and tests hypotheses, evaluates results objectively, preserves failures and successes, improves its own research process, and eventually transfers complete state to a more capable future system.

**2. Current technical state**
- GitHub is the canonical repository for code, state, experiments, and history (verified).
- A scheduled/manually-triggered GitHub Actions workflow runs research cycles (verified).
- OpenAlex-based collector (`fetch_research.py` v2) works: one successful cycle collected 30 papers with zero errors (verified).
- `analyze_research.py` produces `research-assessment.json` and `proposed-next-problem.json` (verified).
