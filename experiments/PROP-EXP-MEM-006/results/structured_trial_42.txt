## 3. Failures and lessons
- **arXiv collector failed** (papers=[], timeouts, HTTP 429) → replaced with OpenAlex-based fetch_research.py v2. Lesson: switch sources rather than retry a rate-limited one.
- **Missing assessment files** because analyze_research.py hadn't been uploaded/executed → analyzer added, workflow rerun. Lesson: verify all pipeline components are deployed before expecting outputs.
