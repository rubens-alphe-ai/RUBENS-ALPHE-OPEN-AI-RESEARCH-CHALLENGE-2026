# Outreach pre-registration, version 2

Written 2026-09-26, before any message was sent. It supersedes the list in
[version 1](PREREGISTRATION.md), which said that any change after its amendment
would be a new pre-registration. This is that new pre-registration.

**Everything in version 1 about tiers, outcomes, power and what messages may
claim stands unchanged.** This version replaces only the list, its groups and
its fingerprints, and it is where the record is kept from now on.

## Why the list changed again

Preparing the messages for sending meant finding each organisation's official
contact channel, and that search found a fact the list had missed: one of the
evaluation vendors on it had been acquired by a frontier model developer in
March 2026 and folded into that company's enterprise platform. A licence
offered to an independent vendor was no longer an offer anyone independent
could accept. It is replaced by an AI governance vendor that the buyer study
had already ranked first.

One contact is now reached through its French practice, so its message is
written in French. Every message is complete: links, signature and channel are
filled in, and nothing is left for the sender to edit.
