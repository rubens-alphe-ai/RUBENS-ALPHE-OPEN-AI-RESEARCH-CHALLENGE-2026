### Amended before the first send

The list first committed here (sha256 `0e2f9a9c…4b34a5e`) was revised on
2026-09-26, before any message was sent, because it rested on a false premise.
It treated the EU AI Act's high-risk obligations as applying from August 2026.
The Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force since 27 July 2026,
moved them to 2 December 2027, and to 2 August 2028 for AI in regulated
products; and no body had yet been designated to assess AI Act conformity. The
three contacts chosen as "obliged" were not obliged to anything yet.

Two conformity bodies and one evaluation vendor for whom no need or budget
signal was found were replaced by three organisations that already sell AI

## What counts

| Tier | What it is |
|---|---|
| 0 | No reply, an automatic reply, or a decline that does not engage with the finding |
| 1 — read it | A reply that engages with a specific number, item, method or limit — something only answerable by having read the finding |
| 2 — tried it | They run a tool on their own data, send data, or ask for the input format |
| 3 — would pay | They ask for a price, a quote, a contract or an invoice, or propose paid work |

Tier 1 is a judgement. Each is recorded with a short quotation, so anyone can
disagree with the call.

## What each outcome means, decided now

Verdict **21 days after the tenth message is sent**. Send dates are appended to
this file as they happen, before the verdict is written.

- **Demand shown** — at least one tier-3, or at least two tier-2. Build the
  sales process and widen the contact list.
- **Interest without demand** — at least three tier-1 and nothing higher. The
  finding lands and does not become a purchase. Next: a smaller paid first step,
  and group A before the others.
- **Not yet known** — anything less.

**"Stop" is not an available verdict at ten contacts, and that is deliberate.**
An earlier conversation about this plan said that zero replies from ten would
be the signal to stop. The arithmetic says otherwise:

| True rate of substantive replies | Chance of at least 3 of 10 | Chance of 0 of 10 |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | 0.01 | 0.60 |
| 10% | 0.07 | 0.35 |
| 20% | 0.32 | 0.11 |
| 30% | 0.62 | 0.03 |

At a true rate of 10% — a respectable one for unsolicited messages — ten
contacts produce no reply at all about a third of the time. Silence from ten is
therefore not evidence against the product, and reading it as such would be the
same error this project catches in other people's leaderboards: treating an
ordering the sample cannot support as a finding. Ten contacts can confirm
interest; they cannot refute it.

So **not yet known** is followed by 30 more contacts under the same tiers
before any conclusion. At 40 contacts and a true rate of 10%, the chance of
seeing at least three substantive replies is 0.78; at 5% it is 0.32, which is
the honest size of what even forty can rule out.

Tallies are reported per group as well as pooled. A result carried entirely by
one group is a result about that group.
