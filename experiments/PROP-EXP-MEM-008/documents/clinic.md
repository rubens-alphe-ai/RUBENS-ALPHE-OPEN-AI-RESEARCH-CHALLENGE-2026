# Handover: Saint-Aubin community clinic, nursing coordination

The clinic serves 4,200 registered patients and runs 6 consultation rooms. Opening hours are
08:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and 09:00 to 13:00 on Saturday. Twelve nurses work in three shifts,
plus four administrative staff. The clinic has held its current accreditation since April 2022;
it expires in April 2027 and renewal requires an audit of infection-control records.

Attendance averages 180 consultations a day, with a peak of 310 during the January 2025 flu
wave. No-show rate is 9 percent, down from 14 percent after reminder texts were introduced in
September 2024.

Two incidents shaped current practice. In March 2024 a vaccine fridge failed overnight and
2,100 euros of stock was discarded; the fridge now has a networked temperature alarm checked
every morning, and a paper log is kept as a backup because the alarm itself failed silently
once. In November 2024 a patient record was updated by two nurses at the same time and the
allergy field was overwritten; the record system now locks a record while it is open.

Three rules are in force. A prescription may not be renewed without a consultation within the
past twelve months, after an audit found 38 renewals beyond that limit. Vaccine stock is counted
twice weekly, not monthly. Any temperature excursion above 8 degrees must be reported the same
day to the regional health agency.

The purchase of a second ultrasound machine was proposed in June 2025 and postponed: the budget
committee asked for a utilisation study of the existing machine first, and that study has not
been carried out. It is unknown whether the current machine is saturated.

The nursing coordinator post has been vacant since August 2025 and is covered in rotation by two
senior nurses, which the team considers unsustainable beyond the winter.
