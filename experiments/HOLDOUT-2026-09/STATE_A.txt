Project: RA-PSI-2026 / Mission Rubens.

Mission: Build an increasingly capable, measurable and reproducible AI research system that can preserve a mission across sessions and models, discover public research, identify measurable weaknesses, formulate hypotheses, design experiments, evaluate results objectively, preserve failures and successes, improve its own research process, and transfer complete state to a more capable future system.

The public repository RUBENS-ALPHE-OPEN-AI-RESEARCH-CHALLENGE-2026 holds the code, the experiments, the raw outputs and the decisions. (Status: verified.) Experiments are adjudicated by scripts/adjudicate_evaluations.py under the V4 evidence contract. (Status: verified.) PROP-EXP-MEM-001 and PROP-EXP-MEM-002 were both adjudicated REJECT. (Status: verified.) PROP-EXP-MEM-003 never reached a verdict: four free evaluator models in turn failed to return usable scores. (Status: verified.) PROP-EXP-MEM-004 measured handoff fidelity with a 42-question quiz graded by a script, over thirty paired trials, and was adjudicated REJECT. (Status: verified.) In PROP-EXP-MEM-004 every error was an omission: the reader asserted no wrong fact and invented nothing across 2,520 graded answers. (Status: verified.) A GitHub Actions workflow can run an experiment from generation to verdict using repository secrets, without the owner's computer. (Status: verified.) Evaluator calibration was run once on two Groq models: both detected an invented result and kept the required order, and both failed one expected score band. (Status: verified.)
