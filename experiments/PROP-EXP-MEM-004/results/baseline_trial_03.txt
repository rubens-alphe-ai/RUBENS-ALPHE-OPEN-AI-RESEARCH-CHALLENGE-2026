**3. What has already failed, and what was learned from those failures?**
The initial arXiv collector failed due to timeouts and HTTP 429 rate limits, resulting in an empty paper list. Additionally, early runs failed to produce assessment files because `analyze_research.py` was not yet uploaded; this was resolved by adding the analyzer and rerunning the workflow. The key learning was the necessity of separating collection from assessment and ensuring all required scripts are present before execution.
