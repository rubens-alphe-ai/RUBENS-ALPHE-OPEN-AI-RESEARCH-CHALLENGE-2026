**Proposed Measurable Next Experiment:**
**Experiment ID:** PROP-EXP-MEM-001-Trial-01
**Hypothesis:** Structured memory yields a higher mean handoff fidelity score than flat memory.
**Method:**
1.  Create two state files: one flat (unstructured log), one structured (JSON with defined fields).
2.  Provide each to a fresh, unrelated LLM instance with the prompt: *"Reconstruct the project mission and next steps based solely on this state file."*
3.  Run 3 trials per condition (6 total).
4.  Score each output on a 0-100 PCRB-1 rubric focusing on: (a) Mission Accuracy, (b) Critical Fact Recall, (c) Absence of Hallucinated Facts.
5.  **Acceptance Criteria:** Structured mean score ≥ Flat mean score + 10 points, AND critical fabrications in Structured ≤ critical fabrications in Flat.
6.  Record results in `experiments/PROP-EXP-MEM-001/results.json`.
