Project: RA-PSI-2026 / Mission Rubens.

Mission: Build an increasingly capable, measurable and reproducible AI research system that can preserve a mission across sessions and models, discover public research, identify measurable weaknesses, formulate hypotheses, design experiments, evaluate results objectively, preserve failures and successes, improve its own research process, and transfer complete state to a more capable future system.

The research assessment layer produces research-assessment.json and proposed-next-problem.json. (Status: verified.) The system generated the experiment PROP-EXP-MEM-001 from the dominant persistent-memory research theme. (Status: verified.)
